Privacy Policy

How DXC Group Inc collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

DXC Group Inc. (“DXC Group”, “we”) is a California corporation. This policy explains what information we collect through dxcgroupinc.com and in the course of client engagements.

Information we collect

  • Contact messages. When you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Basic site data. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Fonts. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Demo tools. The demos run in your browser. Text you enter is not sent to our servers.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use it

We use contact information to respond to enquiries and to manage client relationships. We do not sell personal information.

Client data

Data we receive while delivering services is handled under the written agreement for that engagement, which sets out scope, access, retention and deletion.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. California residents may have additional rights under applicable law.

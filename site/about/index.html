A small, independent firm

DXC Group Inc. is a California corporation founded in 2016. We help operations teams and online sellers remove repetitive work with AI-assisted workflows.

Who we are

We are a small consultancy that works directly with each client. You talk to the people doing the work, not an account layer.

We deliver remotely, which keeps overhead low and lets us work with teams anywhere in the United States.

What we do not do

  • We do not promise headcount reductions or specific savings before measuring your process.
  • We do not automate irreversible actions without a review step.
  • We do not resell client data or use it for anything outside the engagement.
  • We do not take vendor commissions, so our recommendations stay independent.

Certifications

We do not currently hold SOC 2 or similar attestations. Our security practices are described plainly on the security page, and we agree data handling terms with each client in writing.

How we work

A short, staged process. You see the scope and price before work starts, and you can stop after any stage.

Process

Four stages

01 · Assess

We map one process end to end: the steps, volumes, error cases and who touches what. We sample real inputs and measure how long the work takes today.

  • Findings document with a baseline of the current process
  • Recommendation: automate, change the process first, or leave as is
  • Written scope and price for the next stage

02 · Prototype

We build a working version on your real samples, and compare its output with what your team produces today.

  • Working prototype and a results comparison
  • List of exception cases found
  • Go / no-go recommendation

03 · Deploy

We connect the workflow to your systems with human review switched on, logging enabled and a clear way to pause it.

  • Production workflow with review queue and audit log
  • Runbook: monitor, pause, roll back
  • Hand-over session for your team

04 · Improve

Each month we review exceptions and errors with you, tighten rules and report against the baseline.

  • Monthly review of exceptions and results
  • Updated rules and documentation
  • Report against the original baseline

Invoice & PO matching

Paste purchase orders and supplier invoices. The tool matches them by PO number, checks amounts against a tolerance and lists only what needs a person.

Demo with sample data. It runs entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Production versions read PDFs and email, connect to your ERP and keep an audit log.

InvoicePOStatusReason

How it works

  • Exact PO lookup. Each invoice is joined to the order it names.
  • Checks. Unknown PO, supplier mismatch, amount outside tolerance and duplicate invoices against the same PO.
  • Exceptions only. Clean matches are posted automatically; everything else is routed to a reviewer with the reason attached.

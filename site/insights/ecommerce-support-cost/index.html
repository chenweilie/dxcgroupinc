Reducing e-commerce support cost without hurting quality

Practical steps for cutting repetitive support work in an online store while keeping answers accurate.

DXC Group Inc. · 2026-10-30

Support cost in an online store grows with order volume, but much of the work is repetitive. The aim is to free agents from the repeats so they can handle the cases that need care.

Start by measuring

Export a few weeks of tickets and group them by reason. Most stores find a small number of reasons, such as order status, returns and address changes, make up most of the volume. That is where to begin.

Triage before you answer

Sorting by intent and urgency means billing errors and damaged goods reach an agent first, while simple status questions can follow a standard path.

Draft, do not auto-send

Generating a draft from your policies and help articles saves typing time, and the agent still checks facts such as order details before sending.

Fix the source of repeat questions

If many customers ask where their order is, the confirmation email or tracking page may be unclear. Removing the cause beats answering faster.

Write the returns playbook

Clear rules for when to refund, replace or escalate remove guesswork, shorten handling time and make quality more even across agents.

Track the right measures

Watch time to first reply, how often a customer needs a second contact and customer satisfaction, not just cost per ticket. Cost cuts that hurt those measures are not savings.

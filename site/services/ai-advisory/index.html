AI adoption advisory

For leaders who are being asked about AI and want a practical answer: where it helps, what it costs and how to try it safely.

Typical problems

  • Many ideas, no way to rank them
  • Vendor demos look the same and claims are hard to compare
  • Unclear who is responsible if an AI output is wrong
  • Pilots that never reach daily use

What we build

Use-case ranking

List candidate uses and score them on value, effort, data readiness and risk.

Pilot roadmap

A small first pilot with a success measure, a time box and a stop rule.

Vendor and build review

Compare options against your needs, data terms and total cost.

Review and rollout rules

Who checks what, when to escalate and how to log decisions.

What you receive

  • Ranked list of use cases with the reasoning shown
  • One-page pilot plan: scope, measure, timeline, owner
  • Vendor or build comparison where relevant
  • Draft review and escalation guidelines

What we need from you

  • Time with the people who run the processes in question
  • A short description of your data and any constraints on sharing it
  • A decision-maker for the pilot

Limits

  • We give an independent view and will say when the answer is “not yet” or “not with AI”.
  • Legal and compliance review of your use remains with your own counsel.

Business workflow automation

For teams whose work moves between inboxes, PDFs, spreadsheets and several systems, with people re-typing the same data at every step.

Typical problems

  • Invoices and orders are keyed in by hand and checked against another system
  • Emails and attachments are sorted by someone reading each one
  • Month-end reconciliation depends on copying between spreadsheets
  • Errors are found late, and nobody can say where they started

What we build

Intake

Read email, PDFs and forms and pull out the fields that matter.

Check

Compare against purchase orders, price lists or master data and apply your rules.

Route

Send clean items straight through and exceptions to the right person with the reason.

Record

Post to your ERP, CRM or sheet and keep an audit log of what happened.

What you receive

  • Process map of the current workflow and its exception cases
  • Working automation connected to your systems, with review queue
  • Rules and prompts documented in plain language
  • Runbook: how to monitor, pause and roll back

What we need from you

  • A sample of real inputs, with sensitive details removed where possible
  • Someone who knows today’s process and its exceptions
  • Access to the target system, scoped to what is needed

Limits

  • It works best where rules can be written down. Judgement-heavy decisions stay with people.
  • Inputs of very poor quality, such as illegible scans, will still need a person.

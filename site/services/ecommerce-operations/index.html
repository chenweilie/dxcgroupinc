E-commerce operations

For online sellers whose costs grow with every order: support tickets, product data, returns and the exceptions in between.

Typical problems

  • Support agents answer the same ten questions all day
  • Product titles, attributes and descriptions differ across channels
  • Returns and refunds are handled case by case, with no playbook
  • Peak season means temporary hires and uneven quality

What we build

Ticket triage

Classify by intent and urgency, so urgent and billing issues surface first.

Drafted replies

Draft answers from your policies and help articles, for an agent to edit and send.

Listing cleanup

Normalize titles, attributes and descriptions to your own style guide.

Returns playbook

Turn case-by-case handling into clear rules with escalation paths.

What you receive

  • Review of ticket types and where time is spent
  • Triage and draft-reply workflow in your help desk
  • Style guide and cleanup rules for product data
  • Returns and exceptions playbook

What we need from you

  • An export of recent tickets, with customer details removed
  • Your policies for shipping, returns and refunds
  • An agent or lead who can review drafts early on

Limits

  • Replies are drafts. We do not recommend sending AI-written answers to customers without review.
  • Gains depend on how repetitive your tickets are. We measure that in the assessment first.

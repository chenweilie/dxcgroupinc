Approach

Small, measured, reversible

  1. Assess

    A short, fixed-scope review of one process: steps, volumes, error cases and who touches what.

  2. Prototype

    A working version on real samples, so you judge results rather than slides.

  3. Deploy

    Rolled out with human review on, logging on and a clear way to turn it off.

  4. Improve

    We review the exceptions together each month and tighten the rules.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. All engagements are delivered remotely through video calls, shared documents and your existing ticketing tools.

Which processes are a good fit for AI automation?

High-volume, rules-heavy work with semi-structured inputs such as invoices, orders, support tickets and contracts, where a person can review exceptions.

How do you handle sensitive data?

We scope data access to the minimum needed, prefer your own accounts and environments, and agree handling terms in writing before work starts. See our security practices.

How does pricing work?

We start with a fixed-scope assessment. Build and support work is then quoted per project or as a monthly retainer.

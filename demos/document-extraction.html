Contract information extraction

Paste contract text. The tool pulls out the parties, key dates, amounts and clauses into a structured record, and marks anything it could not find.

Demo with a made-up sample agreement and pattern rules. It runs entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Production versions read scanned PDFs, use a language model for varied wording and send low-confidence fields to a reviewer.

FieldValueStatus

How it works

  • Extract. Parties, dates, amounts, term, notice period and governing law become named fields.
  • Flag gaps. Missing fields are shown as missing, never guessed.
  • Output. Records can go to a spreadsheet, database or your contract system, with a link back to the source text.

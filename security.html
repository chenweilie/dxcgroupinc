Security Practices

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This page describes how we approach security when working with client data. It is a statement of practice, not a certification. We do not currently hold SOC 2 or similar attestations.

Principles

  • Minimum access. We request only the data and permissions an engagement needs.
  • Your environment first. Where possible, work runs in accounts and systems you own and control.
  • Written terms. Data handling, retention and deletion are agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Human review. Automated workflows we build route exceptions and sensitive actions to a person, and keep logs.

Third-party AI services

If a solution uses an external AI provider, we tell you which one, what data is sent and under which provider terms, and we use options that fit your requirements.

Reporting a concern

To report a security issue, email sales@dxcgroupinc.com.
